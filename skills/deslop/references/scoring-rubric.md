# Scoring rubric

Score the passage 1-10 on each dimension. Total below 35/50 means it needs a rewrite pass; above that, spot-fix only the dimensions that scored low.

| Dimension | What it measures | Low score looks like | High score looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Directness** | Does it say the thing, or hedge/pad around it? | Stacked qualifiers, throat-clearing before the point | States the claim, then supports it |
| **Rhythm** | Sentence-length variance, punctuation variety | Uniform sentence lengths, dash-heavy | Mix of short and long, varied punctuation |
| **Trust** | Specific claims vs. vague plausible-sounding ones | Abstract hypotheticals, no numbers or names | Concrete examples, real specifics |
| **Authenticity** | A stance/voice, vs. flat neutral competence | Never takes a position, hedges every claim | Has an opinion, commits to a read |
| **Density** | Information per sentence, vs. padding | Filler phrases, rule-of-three padding, restated points | Every sentence earns its place |

## How to apply

1. Score all five dimensions independently — don't let one bad dimension drag down your read of the others.
2. Sum them. **Below 35/50: full rewrite pass** using `patterns.md` and `structural-checks.md`. **35 and above: spot-fix** only the dimensions that scored 6 or below.
3. Re-score after rewriting to confirm the fix actually moved the number, not just changed the words.

This rubric measures the passage as a whole — it doesn't replace scanning for specific tells (`patterns.md`, `vocabulary-2026.md`, `structural-checks.md`), it decides how aggressive the rewrite needs to be.
